VERBILUME

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

The short version. Verbilume does not collect anything about you. It has no internet permission, no account, no analytics and no advertising. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Verbilume does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. The app is built without the Android INTERNET permission, so it is technically incapable of sending your information anywhere.

What is stored on your device

To make learning work, the app keeps a small amount of information locally, inside its own private storage area that other apps cannot read:

WhatWhyWhere
Your display name, level, daily goal, theme, preferred realms, reminder timeTo remember your settingsLocal preferences file
Your review history — cards, intervals, streak, XP, bookmarks, typing recordsTo schedule your reviews and show your statisticsLocal SQLite database

None of this leaves your device unless you choose to export it.

Permissions we ask for

  • Notifications — used only for the optional daily study reminder you switch on yourself. If you never turn it on, the app never asks.
  • Vibration — used for the small haptic pulse when you answer a card.

Verbilume requests no other permissions. It has no access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or phone identity.

Exporting and restoring

You can export your entire learning record as a plain JSON file. The file is written wherever you choose through the system file picker, and from that moment it is yours to keep wherever you like. Nothing is uploaded. Restoring works the same way: you pick a file, and it is read locally.

Deleting your data

Open Settings → Reset all progress to erase every card, streak, bookmark and achievement from the device. Uninstalling the app removes all of it as well. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

Verbilume is a general-audience study tool. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Third-party content

The illustrated dictionary, dataset and audio recordings bundled with the app come from the open-source Word Atlas project and are used under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence. They are shipped inside the app and are not fetched from any server at runtime. Full attribution is available on the in-app Licences screen.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new “last updated” date. Because the app collects no data, we do not expect material changes.
